### Tracking purchases, invoices and ACH payments

* **Orders and invoices** are handled through the **Licensee Orders System**.  Once logged in, Hills and Hollows can download statements and invoices for each order.  Use these statements to reconcile amounts debited via ACH from the company’s bank account.
* **License renewals and payments** (including state fees) are managed through the **Licensing & Permitting System**.  The portal sends email notifications when a payment is processed.
* Utah’s administrative rules (R82) require package agencies to authorize an ACH account; if no payment is manually remitted by the due date, DABS will draw the statement total via ACH.  Those details are contained in the rules rather than on a public page, so the practical approach is to download your statement from the Licensee Orders system and verify that the corresponding ACH debit matches.

### Recommendations for Hills & Hollows

1. **Ensure you have active accounts** in both the **Licensing & Permitting System** (for license management and payments) and the **Licensee Orders System** (for ordering, invoices and shipment tracking).  Contact the DABS help desk at (801) 977‑6939 for credentials.
2. **Subscribe to the DABS purchasing mailing list** by emailing [purchasing@dabs.utah.gov](mailto:purchasing@dabs.utah.gov) to receive monthly price‑book announcements and pricing changes.
3. **Use the monthly price books** and **SPA lists** as the authoritative sources for price updates.  The interactive product list is useful for filtering and exporting product data.
4. **Download sales‑analysis reports** each month from the Sales Analysis portal to compare your POS sales with DABS figures.
5. **For ACH reconciliation**, regularly download your statements from the Licensee Orders system and match them against your bank’s ACH debits; contact DABS accounting for any discrepancies.

These steps will ensure you’re using all available DABS tools to manage orders, track invoices and stay ahead of price changes.
