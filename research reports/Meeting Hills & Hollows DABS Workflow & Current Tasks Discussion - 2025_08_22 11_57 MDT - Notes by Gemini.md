### Summary

Shawn Owen, Tessa Barkan, and Dean LaFleur discussed the complexities of the DABC ordering and sales process, identifying challenges with distinguishing order types, manually entering new items into SSCS due to a lack of UPC numbers, and time-consuming in-store transaction processing with Verafone. They also addressed difficulties with credit card processing for restaurant orders, the impact of DABC system changes on profit margins, and the need to automate invoice processing and monthly sales reporting to DABC. Dean LaFleur raised concerns about unpredictable cash flow from Jenkins and unrecovered credit card processing fees, while Shawn Owen aims to streamline billing, inventory, and cash flow processes through automation.
