Monthly DABS SALES REPORT DUE
When are the monthly reports due?
- Package Agency monthly reports: Due on or before the 10th day of each calendar month for the preceding DABS fiscal month; if the 10th falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or state/federal holiday, the report is due by the end of the next business day. Reports are submitted on the DABS-provided form.[1][2]

- Licensing application timelines shown elsewhere in the portal (submit applications by the 10th to be on that month’s commission agenda) are separate from monthly sales reporting and do not change the monthly reporting deadline.[3][4][5]

Monthly reporting — due dates and process
- Package Agency monthly sales reporting
  - What: Monthly Sales Detail Report specifying the variety and number of alcoholic products sold during the preceding DABS fiscal month.
  - When: Due on or before the 10th day of each calendar month; if the 10th is on a Saturday, Sunday, or a state/federal holiday, due by the end of the next business day. DABS provides the fiscal month schedule by July 1 each year and the reporting form.[10][11]
  - Where/How: Submitted on the DABS-provided form per the Package Agency agreements/instructions.[11][10]
- Clarification: The “submit by the 10th” rule shown in record pages refers to application inclusion on the commission agenda and is separate from monthly sales report deadlines.[12][13][14]
- Off-Premise Beer Retailer / Restaurant: The OpenGov licensing portal shows annual audits and licensing steps only; no monthly sales upload function was found there. Any monthly reporting requirements for these license types are handled via DABS systems or instructions outside the OpenGov portal.[13][14][15][16][17][1][12][6]

Constraints and requirements
- For the Package Agency contract FY26–FY28, DABS requires: Surety Bond(s), ACORD insurance with DBAs/addresses, Signed Lease/Premises Ownership, Local Business License(s) before sending the contract for signatures.[3]
- Insurance certificate must include the licensed premises address as instructed.[3]
- Commission agendas and approvals affect issuance/renewals; printing becomes available upon approval/effective dates.[3]
- The licensing portal does not provide a monthly sales upload feature; use the DABS-provided monthly report form and process.[15][10][11][1]

Quick monthly reporting checklist (Package Agency)
- By 10th each month (or next business day):
  - Compile prior DABS fiscal month sales detail (variety and counts).
  - Complete the DABS Monthly Sales Detail Report form for your PA type.
  - Submit via the method specified by DABS (per form/instructions).
  - Retain confirmation/records for audit.
- Each year by July 1:
  - Retrieve DABS fiscal month schedule and the current reporting form from DABS.[10][11]
