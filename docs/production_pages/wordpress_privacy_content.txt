<h2>2. How We Use Your Information</h2>
<p>Your business data is used exclusively for:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Business Process Automation:</strong> Streamlining inventory and pricing operations</li>
<li><strong>Utah Package Agency Compliance:</strong> Ensuring regulatory requirements are met</li>
<li><strong>Data Synchronization:</strong> Keeping QuickBooks and DABS data aligned</li>
<li><strong>Financial Reporting:</strong> Generating required business and compliance reports</li>
<li><strong>System Monitoring:</strong> Ensuring reliable operation and error detection</li>
<li><strong>Technical Support:</strong> Troubleshooting and system maintenance</li>
</ul>

<h2>3. Data Security Measures</h2>
<p>We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your data:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Encryption:</strong> All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)</li>
<li><strong>OAuth 2.0:</strong> Secure authentication with QuickBooks Online using industry standards</li>
<li><strong>Access Controls:</strong> Limited access based on role and necessity</li>
<li><strong>Audit Logging:</strong> Complete audit trails of all system activities</li>
<li><strong>Regular Updates:</strong> Security patches and system updates applied promptly</li>
<li><strong>Secure Infrastructure:</strong> Hosted on secure, monitored servers</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2>4. Data Sharing and Third Parties</h2>
<p>We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your business data. Limited data sharing occurs only for:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>QuickBooks Online:</strong> Via official Intuit APIs for authorized business operations</li>
<li><strong>Utah Package Agency:</strong> Compliance reporting as required by state regulations</li>
<li><strong>Technical Support:</strong> Authorized personnel for system maintenance only</li>
<li><strong>Legal Requirements:</strong> When required by law or court order</li>
</ul>

<h2>5. Data Retention</h2>
<p>We retain your data according to business and regulatory requirements:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Active Business Data:</strong> Retained while the system is in use</li>
<li><strong>Compliance Records:</strong> Retained per Utah Package Agency requirements (typically 7 years)</li>
<li><strong>System Logs:</strong> Retained for 2 years for security and troubleshooting</li>
<li><strong>Backup Data:</strong> Securely stored and regularly purged according to retention policies</li>
</ul>

<h2>6. Your Rights</h2>
<p>As a business user, you have the right to:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Access:</strong> Request information about data we process</li>
<li><strong>Correction:</strong> Request correction of inaccurate data</li>
<li><strong>Deletion:</strong> Request deletion of data (subject to legal requirements)</li>
<li><strong>Portability:</strong> Request export of your data in standard formats</li>
<li><strong>Disconnect:</strong> Revoke QuickBooks integration at any time</li>
</ul>

<h2>7. Utah Package Agency Compliance</h2>
<p>This system is designed to support Utah Package Agency compliance requirements:</p>
<ul>
<li>All liquor inventory data is processed according to state regulations</li>
<li>Pricing updates comply with Utah Department of Alcoholic Beverage Control requirements</li>
<li>Audit trails are maintained for regulatory inspection</li>
<li>Data retention follows state-mandated timelines</li>
</ul>

<h2>8. Changes to This Policy</h2>
