Privacy Policy

Hills & Hollows LLC - DABS Automation System

Utah Package Agency Licensed Liquor Store

QuickBooks Online Integration for Inventory & Compliance Management

PRIVACY COMMITMENT: Hills & Hollows LLC is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your business data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information in our DABS Automation System.

1. Information We Collect

The DABS Automation System processes the following types of business data:

Data Type Source Purpose
QuickBooks Online Data Your QuickBooks account via OAuth 2.0 Inventory management, pricing updates, financial reporting
DABS Excel Files Utah Department of Alcoholic Beverage Control Price updates, product information, compliance data
System Logs Automated system operations Monitoring, troubleshooting, audit trails
User Credentials System authentication Secure access control and authorization

2. How We Use Your Information

Your business data is used exclusively for:

  • Business Process Automation: Streamlining inventory and pricing operations
  • Utah Package Agency Compliance: Ensuring regulatory requirements are met
  • Data Synchronization: Keeping QuickBooks and DABS data aligned
  • Financial Reporting: Generating required business and compliance reports
  • System Monitoring: Ensuring reliable operation and error detection
  • Technical Support: Troubleshooting and system maintenance

3. Data Security Measures

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • OAuth 2.0: Secure authentication with QuickBooks Online using industry standards
  • Access Controls: Limited access based on role and necessity
  • Audit Logging: Complete audit trails of all system activities
  • Regular Updates: Security patches and system updates applied promptly
  • Secure Infrastructure: Hosted on secure, monitored servers

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your business data. Limited data sharing occurs only for:

  • QuickBooks Online: Via official Intuit APIs for authorized business operations
  • Utah Package Agency: Compliance reporting as required by state regulations
  • Technical Support: Authorized personnel for system maintenance only
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or court order

5. Data Retention

We retain your data according to business and regulatory requirements:

  • Active Business Data: Retained while the system is in use
  • Compliance Records: Retained per Utah Package Agency requirements (typically 7 years)
  • System Logs: Retained for 2 years for security and troubleshooting
  • Backup Data: Securely stored and regularly purged according to retention policies

6. Your Rights

As a business user, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request information about data we process
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Portability: Request export of your data in standard formats
  • Disconnect: Revoke QuickBooks integration at any time

7. Utah Package Agency Compliance

This system is designed to support Utah Package Agency compliance requirements:

  • All liquor inventory data is processed according to state regulations
  • Pricing updates comply with Utah Department of Alcoholic Beverage Control requirements
  • Audit trails are maintained for regulatory inspection
  • Data retention follows state-mandated timelines

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes via email or system notification.
