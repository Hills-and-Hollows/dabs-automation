Here's a comprehensive **brand guide** for Hills & Hollows, compiled from all available online resources and anchored in the "Sinclair white and green" style, as well as clear cues from your own websites and market sector:

***

**Hills & Hollows Brand Guide**

**Core Brand Positioning**
- **Tagline:** *Convenience with a Conscience*.
- **Mission:** Stewards of local foods, championing small businesses, and participating in a robust local economy.
- **Location:** Boulder, UT (840 W Highway 12).
- **Contact:** hillshollowsmanager@gmail.com | 435-335-7349

**Brand Values**
- Sustainability & responsibility in sourcing
- Local community focus
- Organic, natural, and honest product offerings
- Friendly, simple, approachable

***

**Visual Brand Elements**

**1. Logo & Iconography**
- No standardized logo found online, but branded merchandise such as stickers, magnets, and tote bags are offered, suggesting use of a simple, friendly font or wordmark.[1]
- Icons/graphics should remain simple and direct, serving to support—never overpower—the message.[2]

**2. Color Palette**
Based on "Sinclair white and green":[3][4][5]
- **Sinclair Green:** #008000 (primary green, used for the recognizable Sinclair dinosaur, adapted here for nature/local connection)
- **Sinclair White:** #FFFFFF (backgrounds, whitespace for simplicity and purity)
- **Sinclair Red (accents only):** #FF0000 (consider as only a minor supporting color—Sinclair Oil red can be used sparingly)
- **Black:** #000000 (for text, clarity)
- **Supporting Neutrals:** Light gray or "natural" kraft brown for subtle backgrounds, echoing earthy, organic roots

**3. Typography**
- **Headers & Logos:** Bold, clean sans-serif (e.g., Montserrat, Gotham, or Open Sans)
- **Body:** Humanist sans-serif (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman for longer content)
- Use uppercase for key headers/titles; lowercase or sentence case for body and casual messaging.[2]

**4. Photography & Imagery**
- Use authentic, natural-light photography showing local people, products, and farms.
- Imagery should feel un-edited, honest, community-based.
- Feature local produce, goods, and artisans—highlighting freshness, quality, connection.

**5. Layout & Aesthetic**
- Minimal, clean, and uncluttered, with generous whitespace.
- Use green and white for page backgrounds, headers/footers, and major blocks.
- Accent elements may use eco-friendly textures or subtle background illustrations (e.g., outlines of leaves, vegetables, landscapes).

***

**Brand Voice & Content Guidelines**

- **Voice:** Friendly, plainspoken, approachable. Avoid jargon or “corporate speak.”
- **Tone:** Honest, down-to-earth, inclusive, and focused on community benefit.
- **Sample Messaging:** 
  - "Stop by for organically sourced groceries, general merchandise, camping supplies, household products, liquor, wine and beer, and an amazing selection of locally sourced products..."
  - Highlight sustainability (“Convenience with a conscience”), supporting local, and being a community hub.[6][7][1]

- **Customer Segments:** Travelers, locals, organic/natural shoppers, campers/adventurers, community advocates.

***

**Brand Application Guidance**

- **Website & Apps:** Use primary green (#008000) for menus, calls to action, and highlights; white (#ffffff) for backgrounds; black for texts; avoid clutter.
- **Marketing Collateral:** Consistent use of the color palette and logo/wordmark. Use eco-friendly paper/textures where possible.
- **Product Packaging:** Simple design, predominately white or kraft, with green logo/accents.
- **Social Media:** Authentic photography; posts about local farms, small businesses, seasonal products, and community events.

***

**Reference Examples & Assets**
- **Website Brand Consistency:** hillsandhollowsmarket.com, all product images and text reflect the above ethos and palette.[6][1]
- **Sinclair Oil Brand Color Sample (for green):** #008000 (RGB: 0, 128, 0), White: #FFFFFF, Red accent: #FF0000.[4][3]
